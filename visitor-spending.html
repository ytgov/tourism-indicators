Visitor spending

Monthly credit and debit card spending in the Yukon, based on transactions processed through Moneris payment terminals. This indicator tracks overall spending trends to assess the health of the visitor economy.

Highlights
  • Year-to-date visitor spending reached $99.8 million through July 2026, up 17.3% compared to the same period in 2025.
  • July 2026 was the strongest month on record in this dataset, with $23.1 million in spending - a 37.1% increase over July 2025.
  • March 2026 spending rose 20.8% year-over-year, boosted by Whitehorse hosting the Arctic Winter Games.
  • Full-year visitor spending in 2025 totaled $142.5 million, the first complete year available in this dataset.

Notes on data: Moneris processes approximately one in three (33%) of card transactions across Canada. This data captures a significant portion of the payment card market but does not include cash transactions or payments processed through other payment providers.

Source: Moneris Data Services.
